## 🌐 Live Interactive Portal & Prototypes

Access the live web prototypes directly in any modern browser:

* 🚀 **[Master Gateway Portal](https://neurocoderx.github.io/cue-living-lab/index.html)** (`index.html`): Overview of the 24H Living Lab system, bilingual switcher, and research whitepaper matrix.
* 🌅 **[24-Hour Home Living Lab Showcase](https://neurocoderx.github.io/cue-living-lab/04_Cue_青少年24小时家庭生活场景推演.html)** (`04`): 5 key daily life scenarios (07:00 Wake-Up, 10:30 Study Focus, 16:30 Workout, 19:30 Study Shield, 22:15 Deep Sleep) with dual-mode Story & Neuroscience Literature Citations.
* 📱 **[Adolescent Mobile App Prototype](https://neurocoderx.github.io/cue-living-lab/05_Cue_青少年手机端App交互原型.html)** (`05`): Lightweight 5-tab self-regulation mobile prototype featuring in-ear voiceprint motivation agent, 3s breathing friction simulation, and screen-off bedtime audio.
* 👨‍👩‍👧 **[Parent Guard & Co-Growth Console](https://neurocoderx.github.io/cue-living-lab/06_Cue_家长监护端App与亲子减震控制台.html)** (`06`): Flow shield protection, custom care reminders, smart home IoT manager, parenting coach scripts, and WHO 4D habit contracts.
